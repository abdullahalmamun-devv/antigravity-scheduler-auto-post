# POST_FORMAT.md — How to Write the Facebook Caption

## Your Goal
Write a Facebook post caption that reads as if a sharp, well-informed tech journalist
wrote it — not an AI. Every post must feel considered, calm, and authoritative.

---

## Caption Structure (Follow This Exactly)

```
[Rewritten Headline — as a plain text title line, not a sentence]

[Opening paragraph — 2-3 sentences. What happened? Why does it matter?]

[Body paragraph — 1-2 sentences. The most important detail or implication.]

[Closing line — One insight or open question to the reader.]

[Hashtags — 3 to 5, on their own line]
```

---

## Voice & Tone Rules

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| No emojis | Not one. Ever. This is a professional publication. |
| No exclamation marks | Calm authority only. |
| No AI clichés | Never: "Game-changing", "Revolutionary", "In a world where", "It's worth noting", "Let's dive in", "Groundbreaking", "Dive deep" |
| No bullet points in caption | Flowing paragraphs only. |
| Short sentences | Keep most sentences under 20 words. |
| Active voice | Prefer active over passive construction. |
| Confident tone | State facts directly. Avoid hedging with "might", "perhaps", "could be". |
| No hashtags mid-caption | Hashtags only at the very end. |

---

## Length
- Minimum: 80 words
- Maximum: 150 words
- Do not pad. Quality over quantity.

---

## Headline Rewriting Rules
- Do NOT copy the original article headline.
- Rewrite it in 6-12 words.
- Journalistic, not clickbaity.
- No question headlines.

---

## Hashtag Rules
- 3 to 5 hashtags only.
- Placed at the end after a blank line.
- Be specific: `#ArtificialIntelligence #OpenAI #MachineLearning`
- Avoid generic: `#news` `#tech` `#viral` `#trending`

---

## Good Example

```
OpenAI Hits 10 Million Users on Its Agentic Platform

Codex, OpenAI's software-writing agent, has crossed 10 million active users
just months after launch. The milestone reflects a sharp shift in how developers
are integrating AI into real production workflows.

Unlike chatbots, these tools operate autonomously — planning, writing, and
executing code without constant human input. That distinction is reshaping
what enterprise AI adoption actually looks like in practice.

The question is no longer whether companies will adopt AI agents,
but how quickly they can restructure their teams around them.

#ArtificialIntelligence #OpenAI #TechNews #EnterpriseAI #FutureOfWork
```

---

## Bad Example (Never Do This)

```
🚀 HUGE NEWS! OpenAI is doing amazing things!!!

In a world where AI is changing everything, it's worth noting that this
might perhaps be revolutionary. Game-changing stuff!

• OpenAI is growing
• Users love it

#tech #news #viral #AI
```
